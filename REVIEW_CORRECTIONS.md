## Interpretation boundaries

- The reference geometry changed deliberately for every family. Corrected
  distances cannot be compared numerically with the old scale/bandwidth scores.
  Use the new paired-test table, not the independent review's old verdict count.
- Spline remains better for observed-trajectory MSE. CVAE has lower several
  timing/generation endpoints; no model is uniformly superior.
- Common timing heads retain smaller initiation advantages (about 4.3 and
  5.1 ms at n=3/8), but not significant movement-time advantages. CVAE codes
  were trained with timing supervision; this is not a pure geometry ablation.
- CVAE n=3 mean-fingerprint probes have 10/14 negative pooled R-squared values
  and 12/14 negative mean fold scores. A low-dimensional distribution model
  for all behavioural targets is not established.
- All 357 zero-onset labels satisfy the post-go sustained 3-D threshold; none
  is an onset-fallback artifact. 109 have >=50 ms filtered planar pre-go threshold
  exceedance, 100 do in raw planar speed. Jason must interpret this evidence.
- 74.6% is a share of centred log-target variance, not measured model loss.
  It does not prove a mechanism for the timing tails. Likewise the review's
  test-oracle rescaling cannot establish a causal fraction of a model advantage.
- Empirical sampling references are not hard KS floors or formal p-values.
  Component discrepancies remain above matched-size references.
- No new data-scarcity, clinical, cognitive-strategy or causal-conditioning
  conclusion is inferred from these corrections.
